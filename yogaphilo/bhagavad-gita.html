Text and numbering

The Gītā is chapters 23–40 of the Bhīṣma Parva of the Mahābhārata. Verse numbers follow the common 700-verse text that Śaṅkara commented on. Some editions add Arjuna's question at the start of chapter 13, which gives 701 and shifts that chapter's numbers by one.

The English renderings are short working translations. The three groups of six chapters (action, devotion, knowledge) are a traditional reading aid, not divisions in the text itself.

Images in the scenes

Most images come from the verses themselves: worn-out clothes (2.22), the tortoise (2.58), the lamp in a windless place (6.19), pearls on a thread (7.7), a thousand suns (11.12), the upside-down tree (15.1). The Gītā borrows Sāṃkhya's guṇas and tattvas but places them inside one divine reality. Chapter 13 shows the difference: one knower in every field.
