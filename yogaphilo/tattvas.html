Sāṃkhya-kārikā, verse 3

mūlaprakṛtir avikṛtir mahadādyāḥ prakṛtivikṛtayaḥ sapta ·
ṣoḍaśakas tu vikāro na prakṛtir na vikṛtiḥ puruṣaḥ

“Root nature is not produced. The seven beginning with Mahat are both produced and producing. The sixteen are products only. Puruṣa is neither source nor product.” The groups above follow this verse.

About this scheme

The sequence follows Īśvarakṛṣṇa's Sāṃkhya-kārikā (c. 4th–5th century CE), which Patañjali's Yoga Sūtra takes as its metaphysics. The levels named in the panel (viśeṣa, aviśeṣa, liṅga-mātra, aliṅga) come from Yoga Sūtra II.19 as read by Vyāsa's commentary.

Later texts vary some details, for example deriving the senses from different guṇas or merging the order of the elements with Vedānta cosmology. The diagram is a map of principles, not a timeline of physical events.
