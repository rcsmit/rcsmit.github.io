The Yoga Sūtra, book by book

Patañjali's short text moves in four steps: what yoga is, how to practise it, what practice unlocks, and where it ends. Sixteen animated scenes follow that path, from the rippling lake of the mind to the seer standing alone.

    Numbering and translation

    Sūtra numbers follow Vyāsa's commentary: 51, 55, 55 and 34 sūtras, 195 in total. Some editions split III.22 in two and count 196, which moves the later numbers in Book III up by one.

    The English renderings are short working translations. Most sūtras carry several readings, and the commentaries (Vyāsa, Vācaspati Miśra, Vijñānabhikṣu, Bhoja) often disagree on the details.

    Images in the scenes

    Some pictures come from the sūtras themselves: the clear jewel (I.41), the farmer opening the dike (IV.3), the cloud of dharma (IV.29). Others, like the lake of the mind and the burnt seed, come from the commentarial tradition. Section boundaries on the map are approximate topic groupings.
