How similar are foods, really?

Re-runs the cosine-similarity analysis of Cardinaals et al. (Nutrients 2026) on the Dutch NEVO database, and shows how each design choice the paper fixed moves the results. Every number sits next to the paper's own settings. Subset 25 adds energy, saturated fat, sugars and sodium to the paper's 21. The NZO talk tab re-runs the heatmaps from the related Dutch Dairy Association presentation (Peters & Huppertz, ICNAB-03014).

RDIs from the paper's Table 1, with vitamin E at 12 mg (the paper prints µg). Nutrients to limit use EU reference intakes (Regulation 1169/2011). Cut-off 0.78 is the paper's 90th percentile. NEVO-online, RIVM, Bilthoven.
