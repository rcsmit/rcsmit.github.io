Asia per period

One column per stay. A season runs from November to April, May or June, so every stay belongs to the season in which it started. Days count from the first to the last day of the stay, including days without receipts. Each line is a daily code (category_asia); flights, visa and insurance paid in advance are left out.

Asia rows outside every period

Asia spending dated on a day that falls in none of the periods: often rent paid before arrival, a stopover on the way, or a monthly total dated after you had left. Fix the date in the ledger, or widen a period. Click a row to open it in Entries.

Edit the periods

With a WHEREABOUTS sheet in the workbook (Location, Country, Arrival), every stay outside Europe is a period and lasts until the next arrival. Without it, the periods are made from your cash in a foreign currency: a new period starts when the currency changes, or after 21 days without receipts. Seasons that were entered as monthly totals are split per month, by the currency with the most spending that month. To split a stay by place, or to give it a name, edit the list: one period per line, as name; first day; last day. The list is saved in this browser only.

How each year was spent

Months per year (30 days each): working, at home in Europe without work, and in Asia. The current year runs to the last day in the file. Click a bar to see the income of that year.

Per working month, year by year

Pay per working month (salary divided by the working months of that year), how much net worth grew per working month, and spending per calendar month. A year without work has no bar for the first two.

Per year

A month is 30 days. Work months to cover the year is the spending of that year divided by the pay per working month. One working month pays for is the pay per working month divided by the spending per month. Click a year to see its rows.

Jobs and time off

Every job (stays in a row with Work = TRUE) and every stretch without work, with how much net worth went up or down. Salary paid while not working counts for the last job of that year.

Running balance per source

Balance at the end of every day, including opening balances and transfers. "Total without pension" leaves out the pension savings, money that stays put until retirement. Drag in the chart to zoom in, double-click to zoom out. Click a name below the chart to switch its line on or off; double-click to show only that line. Account numbers are shortened to the last four digits. Year over year puts every calendar year of one line on top of each other, January to December. "vs a year earlier" shows for every day how much more (green) or less (red) there is than on the same date a year before.

Bank statement next to your ledger

Choose the CSV export of your bank, or drop it on the page; several files at once is fine. Rabobank exports and Revolut exports (one per currency, named REVOLUT_EUR, REVOLUT_THB and so on) are recognised; foreign currencies are compared using fx_amount. Every statement line is looked up in your ledger on the source with the same account name. The file stays in your browser.
