Edit the periods

With a WHEREABOUTS sheet in the workbook (Location, Country, Arrival), every stay outside Europe is a period and lasts until the next arrival. Without it, the periods are made from your cash in a foreign currency: a new period starts when the currency changes, or after 21 days without receipts. Seasons that were entered as monthly totals are split per month, by the currency with the most spending that month. To split a stay by place, or to give it a name, edit the list: one period per line, as name; first day; last day. The list is saved in this browser only.

What this is

A dashboard for a home-made ledger: one sheet with every euro, baht, dong and dirham that went in or out, across bank accounts, cash in several currencies, PayPal, Revolut and Wise.

Open the workbook and every tab is worked out in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the file stays on your device.

The workbook

  • ENTRIES (or INVOER): one row per movement, with id, source, date, amount in euros, description, type (income_expenses), main_category, category and category_asia. Foreign cash also has the original amount, currency and rate.
  • TRANSFER rows move money between your own sources: the legs net to zero, so they never count as income or spending. Helper sources such as RECEIVABLES, PAYABLES and DEPOSITS park money that is on its way.
  • STARTING_BALANCE rows give each source its opening balance.
  • WHEREABOUTS (optional): Location, Country (two letters), Work (TRUE/FALSE) and Arrival. A stay lasts until the day before the next arrival. Every country outside Europe counts as Asia.

The tabs

  • Overview: net saved, net worth over time, in and out per month, and where every euro went in any period.
  • Annual report: one year, every year, or the whole period as a report with charts and tables, to print, save as PDF, or download as a web page or Excel workbook.
  • Asia and Asia per period: cost per day per season and per stay.
  • Fixed costs and Groceries: what keeps running and what food costs at home and away.
  • Work: what a working month brings in and how many months of living it pays for.
  • Accounts: running balance per source, year over year, and the TRANSFER check.
  • Entries and Pivot: every row with filters, and your own cross-tables.
  • Bank: match a bank statement (CSV) against the ledger.
  • Sunburst and Curiosities: the spending in rings, and the odd facts hidden in the data.

Most numbers and bars can be clicked to see the rows behind them.
