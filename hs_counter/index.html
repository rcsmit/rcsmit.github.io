2. Settings

Attempt = hips and ankles above the shoulders. Hold = torso within the angle from vertical, ankles above hips, hands below shoulders; with "Also require vertical legs" the hip-to-ankle line must be within the angle too. Without it, tuck, pike and other bent-leg shapes also count. With "Timer starts only when legs are together" the hold (and timer) only starts once the ankles are close together, so the kick-up with split legs isn't counted. Settings from "Video speed-up" onward update the results instantly without re-analyzing.

For strongly sped-up video, raise the frames per second: 15 fps on a 4× video is only ~4 fps in real time.
